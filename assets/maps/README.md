The land geometry in `land-50m.json` comes from Natural Earth 1:50m Land, a public-domain dataset.

Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-physical-vectors/50m-land/
Mirror: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson

The original polygons are combined into one MultiPolygon and coordinates rounded to three decimal places for compact delivery. Geography is preserved; the gallery applies its own vector styling.
