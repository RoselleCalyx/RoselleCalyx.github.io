## Ingredient balance

Recipe requirements are common-harvest portions. One common fruit, berry, bamboo shoot, shiitake, crucian carp, small fish, shrimp or crayfish provides one portion. Chanterelles, porcini, carp, catfish, loach, crab and lotus pods provide two. Morels, mandarin fish and eel provide four; koi six; matsutake eight; golden koi twenty-four. Common ingredients are used first when mixing automatically. Selected ingredients are preferred, and any remaining portions can be supplied by others in the same group. The menu previews the exact consumption before cooking.
