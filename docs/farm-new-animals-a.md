# Farm additions — red panda, raccoon and fennec

The active red panda has been refined to a softer juvenile face and more natural coat colors. Its three v2 assets, updated prompt and validation are documented in [farm-redpanda-v2.md](farm-redpanda-v2.md). The original red panda assets below remain available as the first version.

Generated on 2026-10-10 with the **built-in imagegen tool**, one transparent atlas per species. The existing `assets/farm/fox.webp`, `panda.webp`, `walk/fox.webp` and `poses/fox-sleep.webp` were visually inspected as style and runtime-format references. These source files were not modified.

Idle is 320 × 320 with a 301 px bottom anchor. Sleep is 384 × 384. Each right-facing walk sheet is 2304 × 384 with six equal square cells and a 360 px bottom anchor. Real alpha is preserved. Each species' six walk frames use one common scale; source figures are extracted by connected silhouette before packing so tails and noses are not clipped by generator grid boundaries.

RGB quality is 88 for idle and 86 for motion; alpha quality is 100. The runtime walk sheets are respectively 139,794, 137,442 and 148,888 bytes, all below 150 KB. The export script uses the existing connected-figure extractor in `scripts/export-farm-motion.cjs`.

[Inspected contact sheet](../assets/farm/new-animals/contact-a.webp): rows are red panda, raccoon, fennec; columns are idle, sleep, then walk frames 1–6. The source atlases and final contact sheet were visually checked for species identity, style, complete paws/ears/tails, transparency and distinct leg positions. Metadata checks confirm all nine dimensions and alpha channels. Runtime adoption and movement integration are handled separately.

Source provenance is preserved in `assets/farm/new-animals/source/redpanda.png`, `raccoon.png`, and `fennec.png`, copied unchanged from the generated outputs. Export dimensions, file sizes, extracted silhouette dimensions and scale factors are in `assets/farm/new-animals/manifest-a.json`. Re-export with `scripts/export-farm-new-animals-a.cjs` and the three atlas paths in that order.

## Final generation prompts

### redpanda

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent game animal atlas for a cozy farm website. Create exactly EIGHT full-body renderings of the SAME baby individual in a clean 4-column by 2-row grid (canvas aspect ratio 2:1; square cells), each fully isolated with generous transparent space between animals. Style: premium children's picture-book delicate gouache and watercolor, tactile densely rendered fluffy fur, subtle paper texture inside fur, soft warm light, realistic baby animal anatomy with sweet rounded proportions, large gentle glossy brown eyes, no outlines. Each figure uses about 75% of the square cell. No scenery, no ground, no shadows, no props, no text, no labels, no frame numbers, no borders. Genuine transparent alpha background. All noses, ears, paws and tails must be complete and must remain separated from the next cell. Layout, in row-major order: row 1 column 1 seated calm idle, front three-quarter view with all paws visible, inviting expression; row 1 column 2 curled up sleeping with closed eyes and nose pointing RIGHT, tail wrapped naturally around body; row 1 column 3 WALK FRAME 1; row 1 column 4 WALK FRAME 2; row 2 columns 1 through 4 WALK FRAMES 3, 4, 5, 6. ALL SIX walking frames are true side profile facing RIGHT, same scale, same torso/head placement and same feet ground baseline. Distinct phases of one natural seamless four-legged in-place walk cycle: front near paw forward/back alternating, far legs alternating in opposition, smooth shifting small foot lift and tail follow-through. Frame 6 flows back to frame 1. Keep character appearance consistent across all eight figures. Avoid duplicated leg poses, cropped figures, anthropomorphic clothing, vector/flat cartoon style.
Character: a baby RED PANDA, rusty orange-red fluffy upper body, cream white face mask and cheek ruffs, small rounded white-edged ears, dark chocolate belly and short legs, huge plush tail with alternating rusty-orange and dark-brown rings. Distinct red panda face with a small dark nose. Tail extends behind in walks without obscuring legs; in idle it rests to one side. It is a red panda, not a giant black-and-white panda and not a raccoon.
