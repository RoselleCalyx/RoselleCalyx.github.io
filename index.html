About

I’m Chen Jia — a PhD researcher in medical AI.

I am a PhD researcher at the Technical University of Munich and relAI, working on medical image analysis, trustworthy and reliable AI, multi-view learning, and clinically meaningful AI systems.

I care about AI methods that are not only accurate, but also robust, interpretable, and genuinely useful inside real clinical workflows. Previously I worked on medical report generation, retrieval-augmented methods, and multi-view anatomical correspondence learning for coronary stenosis reporting.

Outside research I look for forests, flowers and dark skies — quiet places that keep me balanced and curious.
