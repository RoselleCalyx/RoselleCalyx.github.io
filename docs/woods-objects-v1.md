# Natural woodland objects · 2026-10-09

The foraging scene now uses fifteen types of transparent painted objects, each with three distinct silhouettes: porcini, chanterelle, fly agaric, shiitake, matsutake, morel, wild strawberry, bamboo shoot, pine cone, rose hip, bayberry, stone, grass, fern and leaf litter. Existing game species, season rules and saved collection counts remain in use.

The built-in imagegen tool generated one three-shape atlas per object type, using the woodland backdrop as the style reference. Exact prompts are in `woods-objects-prompts-v1.json`. `scripts/export-woods-objects.cjs` separates connected silhouettes and exports 45 alpha WebP assets, at most 384 px per edge, totalling about 2.35 MB. `assets/wild/objects/manifest.json` records their paths, dimensions, variants and sizes. `woods-objects-contact-v1.webp` and the fifteen individual contact sheets document the selected artwork.

`js/woods-objects.js` caches textures and renders them inside the existing gameplay bounds. Naturally proportioned images are fitted without stretching. Ground finds receive a random variant once at spawn; three visible stone variants differ in outline, stone grain, fissures and moss. Grass and leaf litter retain their split/reveal animation, and rocks retain their roll movement and shadows. Berry and strawberry textures blend into their existing ripening drawings. Basket and journal icons use the same new artwork. Winter snow follows an image-derived top edge on objects; interactive snow mounds use irregular curves with softened edges. If artwork fails to load, the procedural drawings remain available.

`tests/woods-objects-runtime.cjs` passed: all 45 shapes visibly rendered and differed, four seasons, canvas-click harvest and basket persistence, poison observation rule, moving covers, stable spawned variants, journal images, mobile layout and procedural fallback. Desktop, mobile, mature mushroom samples and winter screenshots were inspected. The user's current woods tab was refreshed and visually inspected; their basket was preserved.
